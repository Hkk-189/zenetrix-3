# Solution: User-Controlled Financial Identity Verification

## What This Solution Does

This system lets people own and control their own identity credentials - like a digital wallet that holds verified IDs. Users can share these credentials with banks and financial companies without showing their actual documents or personal information.

## How It Works

### 1. User Gets a Digital Identity Wallet
- User downloads a mobile app and registers with their phone number
- They verify their identity once (this is the "one-time KYC")
- The app stores their verified credentials securely on their phone

### 2. Smart Protection Against Fraud
- During video verification, the system checks if the person is real (not a deepfake video)
- It detects if someone is trying to use a photo, video, or mask to impersonate someone else
- If anything looks suspicious, it flags it for review

### 3. Sharing Credentials Safely
- When a bank needs to verify the user, they ask through the app
- User sees exactly what information the bank wants
- User approves with their fingerprint or face
- Bank gets a "proof" that verifies the info without seeing the actual documents

### 4. No More Repeated KYC
- Once verified, user can use their digital credential for any bank
- No need to submit documents again and again
- Saves time for users, reduces costs for banks

## Why This Matters

- **Stops deepfakes**: AI-powered detection in Edge Functions prevents fake videos.
- **Protects privacy**: Supabase Row Level Security ensures only you see your data.
- **Saves time**: One verification works everywhere via the digital wallet.
- **Inclusive design**: Works on basic phones, supports multiple languages.
- **Following rules**: Built on secure cloud infrastructure matching Indian regulations.

## What Users Need To Do

1. Download the app and register
2. Complete one-time identity verification
3. When a bank needs verification, approve the request
4. That's it - no more document submissions

## What Banks Need To Do

1. Integrate with our Supabase-powered verification API.
2. Send verification requests through our secure gateway.
3. Receive instant proof verification results.
4. Reduce onboarding time and costs.
